Syntax:   dumpdpp  [-b|-l] [-v] <playprint_file>

          -b                   The playprint is in Big Endian byte ordering
          -l                   The playprint is in Little Endian byte ordering
                               (default: the byte ordering of the file)
          -v                   Be verbose
          <playprint_file>     Generated Digital Playprint file (.dpp)

Function: Dump Magic Lantern Digital Playprint Files.
